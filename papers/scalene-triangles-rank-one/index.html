Orbit closures of scalene rational triangles: rank one and prime denominators

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

The unfolding of a rational triangle is a translation surface, and the closure of its GL(2,ℝ)-orbit has a rank, which is one for lattice surfaces. We determine the scalene rational triangles of rank one: besides the known lattice triangles, which form three infinite families and four further triangles, they are the two triangles found by Delecroix and Rüth's computer search, with angles (π/10, 3π/10, 3π/5) and (π/12, π/4, 2π/3), whose unfoldings are Prym eigenforms. We also prove that for a scalene triangle with angles pᵢπ/N, N prime, the orbit closure of the unfolding is a connected component of its stratum. Delecroix and Rüth conjectured that the scalene triangles whose unfoldings do not have dense orbits in their ambient strata form an explicit list. The second theorem proves this for prime denominators, and by the first, every scalene triangle of rank one whose unfolding does not have a dense orbit in its ambient stratum is on their list. The proofs differentiate the holomorphic eigenforms of the unfolding's rotational symmetry along every direction of the orbit closure; at a prime denominator this leaves a statement about subgroups of the units modulo N, proved with character sums, a count of lattice points and a computation.
