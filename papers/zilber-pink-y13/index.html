The Zilber–Pink conjecture for curves in Y(1)3

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

We prove the Zilber–Pink conjecture for curves in Y(1)³. By known results it reduces to a conjecture of Daw, Orr and Papas, that an irreducible curve over Q̄ in Y(1)³, not contained in a proper special subvariety, has only finitely many points whose coordinates are j-invariants of pairwise isogenous elliptic curves. We prove this conjecture by the G-function method, which bounds the height of every such point polynomially in its degree. The obstacle is the places of supersingular reduction, where the period equations involve a trace that changes with the place. Pairing each period equation with its Frobenius conjugate, as in OpenAI's preprint on curves in 𝒜₂, eliminates the place-dependent trace.
