An effective André–Oort theorem for Shimura varieties

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

We prove an effective André–Oort theorem for every Shimura variety. Let Z be a subvariety defined over a number field. For every special subvariety maximal in Z we bound its degree, and the discriminant and level invariant that Klingler and Yafaev attach to it, by effectively computable constants, polynomial in the degree of Z and that of its field of definition. The proof follows Edixhoven, Klingler, Ullmo and Yafaev through Hecke correspondences. For the discriminant and level bounds, a zero-free half-plane for one Dedekind zeta function suffices in place of the generalized Riemann hypothesis, and Cureton and Thorner prove such a half-plane for every number field. In Mumford's moduli spaces of abelian varieties an algorithm lists the special subvarieties maximal in Z from equations for its closure.
