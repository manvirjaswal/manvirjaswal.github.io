Orbit closures of generic rational polygons with at least five sides

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

Mirzakhani and Wright attach to the angles θ of a rational polygon the orbit closure ℳ(θ) of the unfolding of almost every polygon with these angles. They ask whether, for more than four sides, ℳ(θ) can be anything other than a connected component of a stratum of Abelian differentials or a locus of double covers of a component of a stratum of quadratic differentials. It cannot. For every choice of five or more angles, ℳ(θ) is the component of its stratum if the least common denominator k of the angles divided by π is odd, and the locus of holonomy double covers of the component of its quadratic stratum if k is even. When some s of the angles sum to (s − 1)π, Apisa and Aygun treat this case by a different method; our proof does not depend on theirs. We read the rank of ℳ(θ) off characters of the deck rotation of the unfolding and induct on the number of sides by colliding two vertices; where no collision applies, arithmetic modulo k takes its place. We also extend Mirzakhani and Wright's nonvanishing of the second fundamental form from triangles to every number of sides.
