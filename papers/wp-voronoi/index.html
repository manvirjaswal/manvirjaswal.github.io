The maximum of |z2℘(z)| on the Voronoi cell of a lattice

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

Let Λ be a lattice in ℂ, ℘ its Weierstrass function and V its closed Voronoi cell. We show that the maximum of |z²℘(z)| on V is the largest of its values at two half-periods and one vertex of V, using interval arithmetic for a compact set of shapes. We also give, by hand, an upper bound in elementary functions with the same leading term and, for real lattices, the supremum of |t²℘(t)| between 0 and the real half-period. Since x = ℘(z) − b₂/12, this bounds |x(P)|·|z|² at every archimedean place for every point P of an elliptic curve, with z its elliptic logarithm nearest to 0.
