The Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions [after Levent Alpöge]

Abstract

A reader's guide to L. Alpöge's Lean 4 formalization, announced on 20 November 2026, of the Riemann hypothesis for ζ and for every Dirichlet L-function. It states in ordinary notation the five theorems of the archive's Main.lean, then follows the chain of declarations behind them. The chain runs through the reduction to an inequality that excludes zeros to the right of 1/2, the analytic argument through ramp sums and Landau's theorem on windows, the origin of that inequality, and a bound for Tate–Shafarevich groups of Tate twists. The guide compares the classical inputs with their archive forms and sets Alpöge's twenty dated steps against the chain. The theorems and their proofs are Alpöge's, and the guide is a reading of his archive. A. Sutherland has compiled the archive and reports that Lean's kernel and two independent kernels accept it.
