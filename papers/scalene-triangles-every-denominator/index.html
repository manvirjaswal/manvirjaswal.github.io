Orbit closures of scalene rational triangles: every denominator

1 / –
PDF

Abstract

Delecroix and Rüth conjectured that, apart from an explicit list, the unfoldings of rational triangles and quadrilaterals without a reflection symmetry have dense GL(2,ℝ)-orbits in their ambient strata, and proved this for the triangles with angles pᵢπ/N and N ≤ 58. We prove the conjecture for triangles. If a scalene rational triangle is not on their list of three infinite families and nine triangles, the orbit closure of its unfolding is the connected component of its stratum when N is odd, and the locus of holonomy double covers of a connected component of a stratum of quadratic differentials when N is even. With the first paper of this series, it follows that the orbit is not dense in that locus for the triangles on the list, except possibly for the triangle with angles (3π/20, π/5, 13π/20). Most of the proof concerns subgroups of the units modulo N; parts of it, including all of N ≤ 58, are computer-assisted.
